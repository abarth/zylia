# World

## Overview
One paragraph a new writer could read first.

## Geography
Regions, their climates and looks, and how travel between them works. Plan
for travel progression (on foot, then by sea, then by air or equivalent):
what barriers gate each region, in the fiction, and when they open.

## History
A timeline of the events that matter to the story: the past catastrophe or
turning point that explains the present, and what people believe about it
versus what really happened.

## Power
Who rules, who resists, who profits. List factions (one file each in
`factions/`).

## Magic in the fiction
What magic is, who can use it and why, what it costs, how society treats
it. Must fit the magic system in `docs/design/magic.md`, including how
characters learn new spells.

## Everyday life
Food, work, travel, beliefs, festivals, fears. Enough for town NPCs to
talk about.

## Cultures and naming
For each culture: one distinctive custom, its naming pattern with example
sounds, and how its people speak.

## Monsters
Why monsters exist and roam, which kinds live where, and how that ties to
the plot (if at all).
