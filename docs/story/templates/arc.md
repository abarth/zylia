# Story Arc

## Structure
The acts, with what the player can do and where they can go in each. Mark
the open-world stretches and which beats inside them are order-independent.

## Main plot
Numbered major beats from opening to ending. Each beat connects to the next
with "therefore" or "but". Mark the inciting incident, first act turn,
midpoint, low point, climax.

## The antagonist's plan
The antagonist's steps from their side, aligned with the main plot beats.

## Character plots
| Character | Joins | Rising beat | Turning point | Resolution | Ending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Set pieces
One or more per chapter: the specific scene, where it happens, why it will
be remembered.

## Threads
Every setup, mystery and reveal, with where it is set up (at least twice)
and where it pays off. Copy these into canon/threads.md on approval.

## Chapters
The list of chapters with a one-line summary each, target party level, and
the locations each introduces.

## Ending
What happens to the world and to each character.
