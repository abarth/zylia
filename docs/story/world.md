# The World of Zylia

**Status: Seed ideas.** Nothing here is decided. These are prompts for the
first world-design pass (STY-1). Keep what works, replace what doesn't.

## Premise seeds

Pick or combine one as the foundation:

1. **The Singing Crystals.** Zylia's magic comes from crystals that hum at
   frequencies only a few can hear. An empire has learned to tune them into
   weapons, and the crystals are going silent one by one. (Classic FF4 shape;
   risk: too close to FF crystals.)
2. **The Stitched World.** Zylia was once several worlds, sewn together by
   ancient mages. The seams are fraying; places from the other worlds leak
   through. Great hook for varied regions and a "world splits" midpoint
   (FF6's World of Ruin shape).
3. **The Last Tide.** The seas are receding every year, exposing drowned
   cities and old machines. Kingdoms fight over the new land; something
   beneath the sea wants to come back.

## Things the world needs

- **Geography**: 3-4 continents or regions with distinct climates and
  cultures, a reason to need a ship, and a reason to need an airship later.
- **Powers**: an empire or ruling order, a resistance or rival kingdom, a
  religious or scholarly faction, and outsiders (nomads, pirates, a hidden
  people).
- **History**: a past catastrophe that explains the current conflict and
  foreshadows the villain's goal.
- **Magic in the fiction**: why some people can use magic, why it's rare or
  controlled, and how it connects to the magic system chosen in
  [../design/magic.md](../design/magic.md) (e.g. relics as bound spirits).
- **Everyday life**: what people eat, how they travel, what they fear.
  Town NPC dialogue draws from this.
