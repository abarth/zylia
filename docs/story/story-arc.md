# Story Arc

**Status: Proposed structure.** The plot itself is not written yet (STY-1).

## Structure

A two-halves structure, borrowing FF6's shape, suits an open world:

**Act 1: The Small World (linear, ~30%).** The party forms around a local
crisis that turns out to be part of something larger. The world opens
region by region; the player gets a ship near the end of the act.

**Act 2: The Wide World (branching, ~40%).** The party pursues the
antagonist across the world, recruiting the rest of the cast. Several
regions can be done in any order. Ends in a catastrophe: the antagonist
wins, the world changes, the party is scattered.

**Act 3: Reunion (open, ~30%).** In a changed world, the player gets an
airship and chooses the order in which to find the party again. Each
reunion is that character's personal plot climax. The final dungeon opens
once enough of the party is back, and the ending reflects who was found.

## Per-character plots

Each playable character's personal plot should:

- be introduced when they join (their want and wound are visible);
- have a mid-point beat in act 2 that complicates it;
- climax in act 3, ideally in a place tied to their past, and pay off in
  the ending.

## Open questions

- Is the catastrophe in act 2 a world change (map changes) or a personal one?
  A world change costs a second version of many maps.
- How dark is the tone? FF4 and FF6 both have deaths and sacrifice.
