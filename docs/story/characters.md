## Design template

Copy this for each playable character.

```markdown
### <Name>

- **Role in the party**: job, combat role (tank, healer, damage, utility),
  unique battle command.
- **Look**: silhouette, colors, one memorable detail (matters for sprites).
- **Age, origin, faction**:
- **Want** (external goal) / **Need** (what they must learn):
- **Wound**: the past event that shapes them.
- **Arc**: where they start, the turning point, where they end.
- **Personal plot**: the side quest or chapter centered on them, and where
  it falls in the story arc.

```

## Cast shape

Aim for 8-10 playable characters, like FF6, so the party can be varied and
every character can own a chapter. Make sure the roster covers:

- a front-line fighter, a healer, an offensive caster, a fast utility
  character (thief/scout), and a few who blend roles;
- a range of ages (a child and an elder, FF4's Palom/Porom and Tellah);
- at least one character with ties to the antagonist;
- at least one character who joins late and changes the party dynamic.

## Seed concepts

Starting points only.

1. **The deserter knight**: served the empire, walked away after an order
   they wouldn't follow (FF4's Cecil shape). Front-line fighter.
2. **The runaway healer**: raised in a temple that uses healers as political
   tools. Healer; unique command: Pray.
3. **The cartographer thief**: maps forbidden places and sells the maps.
   Scout; unique command: Steal / Survey.
4. **The twin apprentices**: two young mages who can combine spells.
5. **The old engineer**: builds tools from recovered ancient machines.
   Utility; unique command: Tools.
6. **The exiled prince or princess** of a drowned kingdom.
7. **The beast-speaker**: grew up outside civilization; learns enemy
   skills (FF6 Gau / blue magic).
8. **The villain's former friend**: joins late with knowledge that
   reframes the story.

## Antagonists

A great villain needs a comprehensible goal. Design at least:

- **The main antagonist**: goal, method, why now, how they change over
  the story (FF6's Kefka escalates from lackey to god).
- **The lieutenant**: recurring boss with a personal link to a party member.
- **The false antagonist** (optional): the apparent villain in act 1.
